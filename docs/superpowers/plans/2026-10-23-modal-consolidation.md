## Review focus

- Busy mutation plus Escape/backdrop/header close: all dismissal is gated without releasing focus.
- Nested popover, avatar, iframe, or modal: visual and keyboard ownership agree; one Escape closes one eligible layer.
- Hidden retained chat drawer and inline/fullscreen reader: state survives and hidden content is not focusable or counted as native-webview cover.
- Long title/footer plus narrow/touch viewport: heading wraps, actions remain reachable, scroll stays in the body and safe areas are respected.
- Opener unmounted or navigation replaces the dialog: focus lands in the next logical destination rather than hidden or detached content.
